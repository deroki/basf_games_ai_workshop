📖 About the workshop

The BASF Hub Madrid organised this video game workshop with AI for the sons and daughters of BASF employees. They worked in teams and, in just 1 hour, went from an idea to a game you can play on the computer.

The BASF volunteers Carlos Díaz, Marta Espinosa and Noelia Moreno joined the teams. Each of them sat with one team and worked side by side with the kids: they listened, helped explain ideas to the AI, tested the game together and cheered every improvement. They were one more member of the team!

To play the games again or show them to your family, just visit this page and pick one from the list.
