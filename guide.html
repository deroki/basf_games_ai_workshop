🤖 Create your own video game with AI

A step-by-step guide to do it on your computer. You don't need to know how to code!

← Back to the games

💡 You need a computer with internet and, if you are under 18, an adult to help you create your accounts.

1Install Visual Studio Code

Visual Studio Code (we call it VS Code) is the program where you will work with the AI. It's free.

  1. Go to code.visualstudio.com/download.
  2. Click the button for your computer (Windows, Mac or Linux).
  3. Open the file you downloaded and click "Next" until it finishes.
  4. Open VS Code. You've got it!

2Turn on Copilot, your AI helper

GitHub Copilot is the artificial intelligence that will create your game. It has a free version to try.

  1. Create a free account at github.com (with an adult's help).
  2. In VS Code, look at the bottom right: you will see the Copilot icon. Click it.
  3. Click "Sign in" and log in with your GitHub account. Your browser will open: click "Authorize".
  4. If it offers you a plan, choose Copilot Free.
  5. The Copilot chat opens. Below the box where you type, there is a button with the name of a model (the "brain" of the AI). Click it and pick one of the available ones without a padlock. The free plan gives you a limited number of messages per month, so use them well!

💡 If you can't see the chat, press Ctrl + Alt + I at the same time (on Mac: Control + Cmd + I).

3Download the workshop

Everything the AI needs to guide you is here.

  1. Go to github.com/deroki/basf_games_ai_workshop.
  2. Click the green "Code" button and then "Download ZIP".
  3. Find the file in your Downloads folder, right-click it and choose "Extract all" (on Mac, double-click it).
  4. In VS Code click File → Open Folder and choose the folder you just extracted.
  5. If it asks "Do you trust the authors?", click "Yes, I trust the authors".

4Create your game

  1. In the Copilot chat, type /create_game and press Enter. This wakes up the game-making AI!
  2. Answer its questions. First it asks how many of you are in the team, then what the game is like, who the character is, where the adventure happens and who the enemies are. Some questions are answered by the whole team and others by each player in turn. You can even show it a drawing!
  3. Say "OK". The AI tells you what your game will be like. If you like it, tell it so.
  4. Magic! The AI creates the game and it opens in your browser to play. You control it with the keyboard.
  5. Ask for improvements: faster, more enemies, better drawings… as many times as you like, until you say "That's it, I love it!".

💡 The trick: the better you explain your ideas, the better your game will be. If something doesn't work, tell the AI in your own words!

5Play again whenever you want

Your game is saved in the games folder, inside the workshop folder. To play again, open your game's folder and double-click the index.html file. You don't need internet.
